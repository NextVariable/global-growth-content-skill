## English introduction

WalkPocket is an Android app for keeping places and notes for your next city walk. Mark places yourself, add text notes, and save a map while you have an internet connection. You can then view your saved places and notes offline, or export them as GPX. It is a way to keep your own walking ideas together, especially if you want to revisit them without a connection. WalkPocket does not generate navigation or track your live location, and it is not available on iOS. If familiar map favourites or paper notes already do the job, you may prefer to keep using them. Want to understand the setup? See how downloading a map prepares your saved places for offline viewing.
