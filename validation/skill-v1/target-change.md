**QueueNote helps in-house recruitment coordinators review interview-status CSVs to see which statuses still need checking before the next interview.**

价值表达：Import the status CSV, filter the records and review outstanding follow-ups as you prepare the next interview with your interviewers. QueueNote does not connect to ATS APIs or send automatic emails.

CTA: See how an interview-status review works.
