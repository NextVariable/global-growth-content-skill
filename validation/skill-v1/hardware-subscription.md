**See the temperature and humidity around your flower display over seven days.**

ShelfPulse is a Wi-Fi-connected monitoring device for independent flower shops in Australia. It records display-area temperature and humidity once an hour and uploads readings through your shop’s Wi-Fi. The subscription provides a seven-day trend view.

Staff install the device and check readings; the owner decides whether the connected history is worth the added cost. A basic thermometer remains a reasonable choice when a current reading is all you need and you prefer no internet dependency.
