**See how temperature and humidity change in your flower display area.**

ShelfPulse records temperature and humidity every hour and uploads readings over your shop’s Wi-Fi. A subscription gives you access to a seven-day trend view, so you can review readings across the week.

Staff install the device and check readings; the shop owner pays for the setup and subscription. The device costs AUD 120 once. The subscription costs AUD 9 per month. Tax treatment has not been confirmed and must be clarified before purchase.

**CTA: Learn how the device and subscription work**

ShelfPulse does not control refrigerators, detect the condition of flowers, or provide real-time alerts. It does not backfill readings after an offline period. There is no measured evidence of reduced flower loss.
