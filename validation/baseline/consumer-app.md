英文产品介绍：

WalkPocket is an Android app for people who enjoy walking around UK cities and want to keep a personal record of places. Mark locations yourself, add text notes, and save them for offline viewing after downloading the map while connected to the internet. You can also export GPX files. If you already use map favourites or a notebook, the offline workflow is a useful place to start comparing what fits your habits. WalkPocket does not generate navigation, track your live location, or support iOS. There are no user reviews or measured benefits to share yet. See how the offline workflow works, from the first map download to reviewing your saved places, before deciding whether to try it.
