**See which candidate interviews still need follow-up.**

QueueNote helps recruitment consultants at UK agencies with 10–30 people review candidate interview statuses from a CSV. Import your file, filter statuses, and view a follow-up list before the manager’s Friday check.

**CTA: See the workflow**

The workflow starts with a CSV you provide. QueueNote does not connect to an ATS or send emails automatically.

内部信息主线：围绕“顾问每日整理面试状态、主管周五核对遗漏”解释产品。使用者是顾问，付款者是代理公司老板。现有表格熟悉且无需采购，QueueNote必须用实际工作流证明单独查看待跟进状态是否值得增加一个工具；不能预设表格已经失效，也不能声称节省80%时间。
