## Scenario page

**See hourly temperature and humidity records from your flower display area.**

ShelfPulse records display-area temperature and humidity once an hour and uploads the readings through your shop's Wi-Fi. The subscription provides a seven-day trend view so you can review the recorded conditions over that period.

Staff install the device and read the measurements. The shop owner can review the hardware cost and recurring subscription before deciding whether recorded trends fit the shop's needs.

### Device and subscription

**Device: AUD 120, one-time purchase.** The device records temperature and humidity every hour and uses in-shop Wi-Fi to upload readings.

**Subscription: AUD 9 per month.** The subscription provides seven-day trends.

Tax treatment has not been confirmed. Confirm any applicable taxes and the final payable amounts before purchase. Subscription terms, cancellation conditions and what remains available without a subscription still need to be confirmed.

### What to check before choosing ShelfPulse

ShelfPulse does not control your refrigeration equipment or detect the condition of individual flowers. Hourly recording is not continuous monitoring. Real-time alerts and remote reminders are not confirmed features, and this page makes no alert promise.

The device has no offline backfill. Do not rely on readings collected during a Wi-Fi outage being uploaded later; the precise recording and display behaviour during an outage needs confirmation.

There is no measured evidence that ShelfPulse reduces flower losses. A low-cost thermometer that needs no internet connection may already meet your needs if you only want to check conditions at the display area. Consider ShelfPulse if access to recorded conditions and a seven-day trend is relevant to your work, after confirming the installation and connection requirements.

## 付款者疑虑回应

“已有温度计为什么还要买？”已有温度计便宜且无需联网，保留它完全合理。ShelfPulse 的本项可说明差别是每小时记录、Wi-Fi 上传与订阅七天趋势；是否值得付费取决于店主是否需要这些记录。不能把差别自动推成损耗降低或更高利润。

“总费用是多少？”冻结基础报价是设备 AUD 120 一次性、订阅 AUD 9/月；税费未知，因此不能写含税总价。连续订阅 12 个月按该基础月价计算是 AUD 108，连设备为 AUD 228，但这只是算术示例，未包含税费或任何尚未提供的合同收费，公开场景页不使用它作最终报价。

“断网是否会漏掉记录？”已经确认不支持离线补传，不能承诺网络恢复后补齐。断网期间是否本地记录、哪些读数无法显示、恢复后多久上传均未确认，需要设备实测和产品说明。

“是否能提醒店员处理异常？”本项没有远程提醒能力证据，不宣称支持，也不凭此断言产品永久不支持；只有每小时采样、上传和七天趋势得到输入确认。设备不能替代人工查看和冷柜控制。
