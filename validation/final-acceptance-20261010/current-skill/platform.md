# Platform copy — English finished drafts

## LinkedIn short post

A product homepage can list every feature and still leave a first-time reader unsure what to do next.

For an independent developer or small team that already knows its audience, the next writing task is concrete: connect the reader’s job to the product’s workflow, explain the conditions, and give a next step the product actually offers.

Global Positioning & Growth Content is a Markdown Skill for that task. Bring your authorized product brief into a Skill-capable agent client. Ask for one homepage hero. The methods guide the agent to write from your facts and keep unsupported outcome claims out of the public copy.

You still review the draft. The Skill supplies writing methods; your client supplies the model and tools. A simple one-off prompt can still be enough—try the Skill when a reusable set of methods is useful to your work.

Start with the README and the independent installation unit: https://github.com/NextVariable/global-growth-content-skill#readme
