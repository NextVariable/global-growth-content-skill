# Platform-ready English content

## LinkedIn short post

For developers and small product teams: if you already know who your product serves, your next content task can be one clear first screen.

Global Positioning & Growth Content is a Markdown Skill for turning that existing decision and your product facts into positioning and English content inside a Skill-capable agent client.

Try a homepage hero. Bring the reader, workflow, evidence and page goal; draft the copy; check every claim before using it.

It uses your client's model. It does not publish content or prove conversion gains. The package is MIT licensed; no npm or pip package is required to use its Markdown guidance.

Read the README and get the Skill:
https://github.com/NextVariable/global-growth-content-skill#readme
