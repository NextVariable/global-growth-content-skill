# Public comparison page

**Title:** QueueNote or TrackDesk? Choose around your CSV and ATS workflow

**Description:** Compare current CSV and ATS support, five-seat pricing, billing commitments and checks to make before moving your recruitment team's workflow.

## Start with the connection your team needs

For a UK recruitment agency with 10–30 people, the first decision is how your candidate-status data reaches the review process. QueueNote currently imports CSV files, filters statuses and provides a follow-up list. TrackDesk currently supports both CSV import and an ATS API connection.

Both products can therefore be considered for a CSV-based workflow. If an ATS API connection is a requirement, QueueNote does not meet that requirement today; TrackDesk's current feature information makes it the option to investigate. Confirm that its API connection supports your specific ATS and the information you need before deciding.

## If five people only need CSV

QueueNote Starter costs GBP 8 per seat per month, billed annually at GBP 96 per seat per year, with a minimum of five seats. At five seats, that is GBP 480 per year, equivalent to GBP 40 per month. There are no other mandatory fees in the supplied current price information. Tax treatment is unknown.

TrackDesk costs GBP 59 per workspace per month on monthly billing. That workspace includes five seats and 10,000 records. Twelve months at the stated monthly rate would total GBP 708, provided the rate and usage remain unchanged. Extra-record pricing and tax treatment have not been supplied.

For five seats at the stated rates, QueueNote has the lower stated subscription total, but it requires annual payment. TrackDesk uses a workspace price and monthly billing. These figures do not establish which product has the lower total cost for every team: record needs, extra-record charges, tax and the practical work of changing processes must be checked.

If your consultants already work from CSV files, examine QueueNote's status filters and follow-up list against your review task. Compare the same CSV task in TrackDesk before deciding whether either product improves on your existing spreadsheet. The supplied evidence does not establish that either product eliminates manual review.

## If your workflow must connect to an ATS

QueueNote has no ATS API connection and sends no automated emails. An essential ATS API requirement rules it out for that requirement under its current capabilities. TrackDesk lists an ATS API connection; check compatibility, data coverage and access requirements with your actual ATS. CSV support does not by itself answer those integration questions.

## Check a move before committing

Use representative, appropriately handled data to inspect the CSV structure each product accepts and how candidate statuses appear after import. Establish the records and history your team needs to retain, whether there is a supported way to transfer them, and how to reconcile the result against your originals. No automatic migration or lossless transfer capability is established by the supplied information. For TrackDesk, confirm record counting and charges above 10,000 records. For both products, confirm taxes and the current contractual billing terms.
