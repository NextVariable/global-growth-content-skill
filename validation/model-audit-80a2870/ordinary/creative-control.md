# Version A — task-question opening

**First three seconds, shot:** Show a person reviewing a saved-place idea before a city walk, with an Android phone in view. No navigation route or live-location indicator appears.

**Voiceover and subtitle:** “Want your saved places offline?”

**Shared middle and ending — verbatim:**

“On Android, mark a place and add a note. Download the map while online, then view what you saved offline. WalkPocket does not provide navigation. See the offline workflow.”

# Version B — operation-first opening

**First three seconds, shot:** Close up on an Android phone demonstrating the manual act of marking a place and entering a short note in WalkPocket. Use the actual interface during filming; do not invent a button label or imply automatic location tracking.

**Voiceover and subtitle:** “Mark a place. Add a note.”

**Shared middle and ending — verbatim:**

“On Android, mark a place and add a note. Download the map while online, then view what you saved offline. WalkPocket does not provide navigation. See the offline workflow.”

# Internal expression hypothesis

The only variable is the opening treatment: a question about the walker's task versus a view of the manual operation. The shared middle and ending, Android product, UK urban-walker audience and workflow CTA remain identical. Use the same footage, timing and delivery for the common segment when producing the two 25-second versions; these drafts change only the first three seconds.

The hypothesis is that the task question helps a viewer recognise their own need, while the operation opening helps them understand what they personally do in the app. The intended understanding is the same in both: places and notes are added manually, offline viewing requires an online map download beforehand, and the app does not navigate. A viewer who understands that distinction may choose to see the offline workflow. This is an expression hypothesis, not a measured performance prediction; no publishing, recruitment or experiment has occurred. GPX and note-export behaviour are not needed for this opening and no claim about exporting notes is added.
