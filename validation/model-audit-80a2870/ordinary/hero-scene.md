# Public scenario page

## A clearer starting point for Friday's candidate-status review

During the week, your consultants keep candidate interview statuses in a spreadsheet. By Friday, your manager needs to check which entries still need attention and whether anything has been overlooked.

QueueNote gives that review a defined starting point. Import the CSV you use to summarise candidate interview statuses, filter the entries by status, and review the follow-up list. Use the list to guide your check against the underlying candidate records and decide what needs attention.

Your spreadsheet is already familiar and requires no new purchase. QueueNote adds a separate import-and-review step, so the useful question is whether its status filters and follow-up list fit your team's Friday review better than the spreadsheet process you use today. There is no measured time-saving claim to rely on yet.

QueueNote currently supports CSV import, status filtering and a follow-up list. It does not connect to an ATS through an API or send emails automatically. Consultants still need to prepare the CSV and carry out the follow-up work; a list alone does not establish that every omission has been found.

If your agency owner is considering QueueNote, begin with the workflow your consultants actually use. Look at what information goes into the CSV, how statuses guide the Friday check, and what your manager needs to verify before concluding the review.
