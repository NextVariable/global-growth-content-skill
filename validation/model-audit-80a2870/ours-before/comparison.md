# QueueNote or TrackDesk: choose around your recruitment workflow

For a UK recruitment agency with 10–30 people, the first question is whether a CSV-based review is enough or an ATS connection is required. Both products support CSV import. QueueNote also has confirmed status filtering and a follow-up list; TrackDesk’s current information confirms an ATS API connection. QueueNote does not offer an ATS API or automatic emails.

## If five people need only a CSV workflow

QueueNote can be considered when your task is to import candidate information, filter statuses and review follow-ups. TrackDesk also supports CSV import, so CSV alone is not a unique QueueNote advantage. The supplied TrackDesk information does not establish whether it has equivalent status filters or follow-up lists; check those requirements before treating either workflow as a substitute for the other.

If your team’s familiar spreadsheet already supports the review, retaining it is also a reasonable choice. Neither a paid workspace nor a new migration is necessary simply because another product can import CSV.

## If your team must connect its ATS

TrackDesk is the relevant option to investigate because its current feature information includes ATS API support. QueueNote does not meet that requirement. Confirm that TrackDesk supports your particular ATS and the data and permissions you need before choosing it; the supplied information does not establish specific ATS compatibility.

## Compare the billing terms before deciding

QueueNote Starter costs GBP 8 per seat per month, billed annually: GBP 96 per seat per year. It requires at least five seats. For five seats, the annual amount is GBP 480, equivalent to GBP 40 per month across those seats. This is an annual billing commitment, not a GBP 40 monthly-payment plan. No other mandatory fees are specified; tax treatment is unknown.

TrackDesk costs GBP 59 per workspace per month, paid monthly. It includes five seats and 10,000 records. Twelve months at that unchanged rate would total GBP 708. Extra-record pricing has not been supplied, and tax treatment is unknown. Pricing for a larger seat count is also not established by this comparison.

The supplied five-seat base figures are GBP 480 for a year of QueueNote and GBP 708 for twelve months of TrackDesk at its current monthly rate. Their commitments and included scope differ. These figures do not establish that QueueNote is cheaper overall for every team or usage level, especially where an ATS connection is required or additional records are involved.

## Check a representative file before switching

Review the CSV columns and status values your team actually uses against each product’s import requirements. Test a representative file and check that its records can support the review you need. Confirm how existing information will be retained, whether the exports you need are available, who can access the records and what manual work remains. These are migration checks, not claims that either product provides automatic conversion, lossless transfer or a specific export feature.

For TrackDesk, also confirm the relevant ATS connection and any costs beyond the included record allowance. For QueueNote, confirm that the annual commitment and minimum seat count suit your team. Keep your current records available during evaluation; neither this page nor CSV import establishes a complete migration path.
