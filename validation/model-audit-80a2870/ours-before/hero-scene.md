# Public copy — scenario page

## Prepare for Friday’s interview-status review

At the end of the week, your manager needs to check whether candidate interview updates or follow-ups have been missed. Your team already knows its spreadsheets, and keeping that familiar process can be a sensible choice: it needs no new purchase and gives consultants a working record they understand.

QueueNote offers a dedicated place to review the CSV information you bring into it. It supports CSV import, status filtering and a follow-up list. The starting point is the candidate-status CSV you compile each day.

### Bring the week’s candidate statuses into view

Import your CSV into QueueNote, then filter the records by status to focus your review. Use the follow-up list to identify records to check against your existing candidate information. Before Friday’s manager review, check those records and decide which follow-ups need your attention.

The list gives your review a starting point. Its usefulness depends on the information in your CSV and on your team checking the underlying records; it is not proof that every omission has been found.

### Keep the follow-up decision with your team

QueueNote does not connect to an ATS API or automatically send emails. Consultants still need to prepare their CSV information and carry out any contact or correction themselves. The manager’s review remains a check by your team; this workflow does not assume shared access or manager permissions within QueueNote.

### Decide whether a separate review workspace is useful

If your spreadsheet already makes weekly follow-ups clear, there may be little reason to change it. QueueNote is worth understanding when you want to assess whether importing a CSV, filtering statuses and reviewing a follow-up list would fit your weekly routine. There are no customer results or measured time savings available yet.
