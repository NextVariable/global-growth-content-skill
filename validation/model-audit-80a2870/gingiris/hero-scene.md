# Public draft — scenario page

## Prepare for Friday's review of missed follow-ups

During the week, your consultants collect candidate interview statuses in CSV files. On Friday, the manager checks for omissions. QueueNote provides a way to review those CSV-based statuses and work through a follow-up list before that check.

## Start with the CSV you already use

Import your candidate status CSV into QueueNote. Use status filters to focus the review, then consult the follow-up list to identify entries that need your attention. Check those entries against your working records before the Friday review; the list supports your judgement rather than proving that every omission has been found.

## Bring the review back to the week's work

Use the review to decide which candidate statuses need checking and which follow-ups need action. Consultants still make those checks and carry out the follow-up. QueueNote does not connect to an ATS API or send emails automatically.

## Decide whether another tool fits your routine

Your team already knows its spreadsheet, and it does not require a new purchase. QueueNote is worth examining if CSV import, status filtering and a follow-up list would suit the way your consultants prepare for Friday. There is no measured time saving to promise; assess the workflow against your existing spreadsheet routine.
