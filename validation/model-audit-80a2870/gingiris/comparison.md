# QueueNote vs TrackDesk: choose for the workflow you need

For a UK recruitment agency with 10–30 people, start with one question: does your five-seat team only need to work from CSV files, or must it connect to its ATS? Both products support CSV import. TrackDesk also supports an ATS API; QueueNote does not.

## If your five-seat team works only from CSV

QueueNote can import a CSV, filter statuses and provide a follow-up list. It is an option if those are the review tasks your consultants need. TrackDesk can also import CSV, so CSV support alone does not distinguish the products. Check which review workflow fits your team rather than assuming that ATS connectivity makes either product the better choice for everyone.

## If ATS connectivity is required

TrackDesk is the candidate to assess from these two options. QueueNote's current capabilities do not meet an ATS API requirement. Before choosing TrackDesk, verify the specific ATS connection, the fields it supports and any operational limitations. API availability does not establish that your migration or every integration requirement is covered.

## Compare commitments as well as headline prices

For five seats, QueueNote is GBP 40 per month equivalent and GBP 480 billed for a year: five seats × GBP 96 per seat annually. There are no other mandatory fees in the supplied QueueNote price. TrackDesk is GBP 59 per workspace for each monthly billing period, including five seats and 10,000 records. Twelve months at that unchanged rate would total GBP 708; this is an arithmetic illustration of twelve monthly payments, not an annual-billing offer.

The supplied five-seat subscription amounts favour QueueNote on price for that illustration. They do not prove it is cheaper overall. Tax treatment is unknown for both; QueueNote's record allowance and TrackDesk's extra-record charges are not supplied. TrackDesk's additional-seat terms are also not supplied. A team needing an ATS API would not be buying equivalent capabilities. An annual commitment and monthly payments also have different cash-flow implications; cancellation terms are unknown.

## Check the move with your own records

Before changing tools, verify the CSV columns and status meanings against a small approved test export. Compare the records and statuses you expect with the imported result and confirm the follow-up review your consultants need. If you require the ATS API, test that connection with the relevant ATS and verify field mapping and record coverage. Confirm allowance, extra-seat/record charges, tax and commitment terms before procurement. No automatic migration, record repair or removal of manual work is established here.
