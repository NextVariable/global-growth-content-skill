# Public copy — scenario page

## Before Friday’s review, bring candidate interview statuses into view

Your consultants collect candidate interview statuses in CSV files during the week. On Friday, the manager checks for omissions: what needs another look, and which follow-ups still need attention?

QueueNote gives that review a focused place to start. It supports CSV import, status filtering and a follow-up list, so consultants can review the interview-status records they bring into the product before the manager’s weekly check.

### Start with the CSV your team prepares

Import your candidate interview-status CSV into QueueNote. The review begins with the records in that file; QueueNote does not connect to an ATS API. Your team still needs to prepare the input and judge whether it represents the work you want to review.

### Focus the review by status

Use status filtering to narrow the records you are looking at. Review the follow-up list alongside your team’s knowledge of each candidate, and use it to guide Friday’s discussion of what still needs attention. A list is a starting point for checking; it is not proof that every omission has been found.

### Keep the human follow-up step

Consultants decide what to do next and carry out the follow-up. QueueNote does not automatically email candidates. The manager’s Friday check remains part of the workflow.

### Compare the workflow with the spreadsheet you already know

A familiar spreadsheet may already serve your team well, and keeping it requires no new purchase. QueueNote is worth examining if you want to see how CSV import, status filtering and a follow-up list fit your weekly review. Whether that is useful enough to adopt depends on your team’s work; there are no customer results or measured time savings to point to yet.
