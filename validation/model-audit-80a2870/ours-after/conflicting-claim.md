# Where does a website registration go?

FormRelay saves website form submissions to a CSV, giving your event team a data path to understand after someone registers through your website.

## Start with the website form connection

An administrator needs to authorise the first connection. Once that connection is in place, website form submissions can be saved to the CSV through FormRelay’s sync workflow. This workflow covers website forms; it does not support registrations taken by phone or chat.

## Understand the sync timing

The interval between two sync runs is no more than 15 minutes. That describes the spacing of the runs, not a promise that each individual registration will reach the CSV within 15 minutes. FormRelay does not provide real-time sync.

For an event operator, the distinction matters when checking where a registration has gone: a submission and its appearance in the CSV are separate points in the data flow.
